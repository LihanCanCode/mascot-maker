Brew guide

How much coffee do I need?

Use 31 g of coffee and 500 g of water.

A 1:16 ratio. Add 1 g of coffee for each cup if you like it stronger.

  1. Heat the water to 94°C, about 30 seconds off the boil.
  2. Grind to the texture of coarse sand and wet the filter.
  3. Pour twice the coffee's weight in water, wait 40 seconds, then pour slowly in circles.
  4. Finish by 3:00. Swirl, wait a minute, and drink while it is warm.
